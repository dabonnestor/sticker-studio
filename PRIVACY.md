# Sticker Studio — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 4, 2026*

Sticker Studio ("we", "us") is a sticker design tool that runs entirely in
your browser. This policy explains what information we do — and more
importantly, don't — collect.

## The short version

**We do not collect or store your designs. We have no accounts, no servers
of our own, no analytics, and no tracking cookies. Everything you create in
Sticker Studio stays on your device.**

## What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. The App does not require an account, does not
ask for your name or email, and does not send your designs or uploads to us.

## Where your data lives

Your working draft and your recent uploads are saved automatically in your
browser's local storage (a per-site storage space on your own device) so
your work survives page refreshes. This data:

- Never leaves your device except when **you** choose to export a design
  (for example, exporting a sticker as an image or PDF, or downloading a
  design file);
- Can be removed at any time by clearing your browser's "site data" for the
  Sticker Studio URL;
- Is lost if you clear your browser storage.

## Third-party services

The App's image search panels are powered by two third-party services:

- **Pixabay** — your search queries and the images you browse are sent to
  Pixabay's API. Pixabay's privacy policy and content license apply.
- **Unsplash** — your search queries and the images you browse are sent to
  Unsplash's API. Unsplash's privacy policy and content license apply.

These requests are made directly from your browser, in line with how those
services' own client-side examples work. We do not see or log those requests.

## What we don't do

- We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information — we don't have
  any to share.
- We do not use advertising trackers or analytics.
- We do not place cookies.

## Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised, and
material changes will be noted on the App's privacy page.
