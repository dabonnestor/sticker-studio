# Sticker Studio — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 10, 2026*

## What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through the contact form on the
landing page: your name, email address, shop name, and the message you
write. We don't run analytics, tracking scripts, or advertising on this
site.

## How we use it

We use the contact form details to reply to your inquiry about Sticker
Studio. We don't sell, rent, or share your information with anyone outside
of processing your message.

## Where it's processed

Contact form submissions are processed by Formspree, a third-party form
service. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy
policy.

## The editor demo

The design editor demo at /editor runs entirely in your browser. Your
designs are auto-saved to your browser's local storage and never uploaded
to our servers. Clearing your browser data removes them.

## Third-party artwork

The editor's artwork search pulls images from Pixabay and Unsplash.
Searching sends your query to those services, and any image you use is
subject to their respective licenses.
